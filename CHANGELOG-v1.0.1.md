### How to Use the New Features:

1. **Start Writing:**
   - Click the **+** button
   - Just start typing - no need to add a title first!
   - The date is displayed automatically

2. **Customize Colors:**
   - Click the **Palette (🎨) icon**
   - Choose from 5 beautiful background themes
   - Select your preferred text color
   - Adjust font size with live preview

3. **Save Your Entry:**
   - Click the **Done (✓) icon**
   - Give your entry a memorable title in the dialog
   - Or leave it blank for "Untitled"
   - Your entry is saved!

4. **Set Defaults:**
   - Click **Settings** from the main screen
   - Choose your favorite theme colors
   - Set default font size
   - All new entries will use these settings
